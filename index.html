About The Practice

Personal first, advisory built around trust

Oikos Advisory is the advisory identity I am building from my work within CASB. The personal brand matters because clients do not only buy policies; they choose the person who will explain, review, and stand with them when decisions become serious.

Rooted in the idea of household protection

The name Oikos comes from the ancient idea of the household: the place where care, provision, continuity, and responsibility begin. That idea shapes the work: protect the people, protect the income, protect the future.

Built around dignity, not fear

Protection planning should not be about scaring people. It should be about giving families time, options, and dignity when life becomes difficult.

Personal stories, practical decisions

The content here will grow from real conversations about youth planning, family responsibility, business succession, and the choices insurance can create.

How I Can Support Your Planning

Life Insurance Planning

Structured protection for income, family responsibilities, debts, and long-term security, matched to your life stage rather than sold as a one-size-fits-all product.

Medical & Critical Illness Review

A clear review of medical card, critical illness, and protection gaps so clients can see where they are exposed before an emergency makes the decision for them.

Business Continuity Protection

Planning for founders, partners, and key people whose absence would affect cash flow, family stability, employees, or the future of the business.

Client Advisory & Education

Plain-language explanation, policy comparison, and decision support so clients understand the reason behind each recommendation before they commit.
